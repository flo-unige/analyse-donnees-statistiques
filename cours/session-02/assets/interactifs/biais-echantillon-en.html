More sites, less bias?

You want to estimate the mean nitrate concentration across 900 fictional sites. Their mean, which is known in this simulation, is 5 mg/L.

Try it yourself: first leave the remote sites excluded and increase the sample size to 500. Does the mean move closer to 5 mg/L? Then make those areas accessible. The sample size returns to five sites. Increase it to 500 again and compare the results at the same sample size.
